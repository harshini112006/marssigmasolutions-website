Independent Verification

Technical rigour.
Zero bias.

Effective quality assurance extends beyond internal checks. Third-party inspection gives you an objective view of whether products, processes and suppliers conform to the specified requirements.

Our inspectors work from your drawings, specifications and acceptance criteria, and report exactly what they find, free from production pressure or supplier influence.

Fewer defects reach customers

Non-conformities are caught before goods leave the supplier or your facility, protecting your reputation and customer relationships.

Less rework and scrap

Checking at the right stage stops defects from travelling downstream, where every correction costs more time and material.

Evidence you can act on

Clear inspection reports with findings and supporting records, so accept, hold or reject decisions rest on facts.

Our Inspection Services

From supplier to shipment,
every stage verified.

Inspection is planned around the points in your supply chain where defects are most likely to start, so problems are contained early rather than found by your customer.

  1. 01 · At the supplier

    Vendor Inspection

    Assessment of supplier capability, quality controls and product conformity at the vendor's site, before parts are released to you.

  2. 02 · On receipt

    Incoming Inspection

    Verification of raw materials and bought-out parts against specifications, drawings and supplier certificates as they arrive.

  3. 03 · On the line

    In-Process Inspection

    Checks at intermediate stages of production to stop defects from propagating downstream and causing costly scrap or rework.

  4. 04 · Finished goods

    Final Inspection

    Confirmation that finished products meet specifications, functional requirements and acceptance criteria before release.

  5. 05 · Before shipment

    Pre-Dispatch Inspection

    Verification of quantity, packaging, labelling and shipping documents so every consignment leaves complete and correct.

How an Inspection Works

Clear criteria in.
Clear decisions out.

  1. 01

    Plan

    We agree the scope, sampling and acceptance criteria from your drawings, specifications and purchase order.

  2. 02

    Inspect

    Our inspector checks dimensions, workmanship, function, packaging and records on site.

  3. 03

    Report

    You receive a clear report with findings, measurements and photographs of any non-conformities.

  4. 04

    Decide

    Accept, hold or reject with confidence, and follow up corrective action with the supplier.
